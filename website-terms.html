Backup Power Pro is a trade name of Key Electric LLC, doing business as Backup Power Pro. These terms describe our website and the texts you choose to receive from Backup Power Pro.

1. Quote requests and estimates

The Quote Walk helps us understand your generator and home connection setup. Its price range is based on your answers and the setup shown. We review your information and setup photos before preparing a firm proposal.

Accepting a range and providing your contact details saves your inquiry. It does not approve installation work, book an installation or make a payment. Sending setup photos is a separate step. Your written proposal identifies the final scope and price for your project.

Quote requests are accepted throughout South Carolina. We confirm the project details and whether we can take on your installation before making a commitment to do the work.

2. Your information and private links

Provide accurate contact and project information. You may contact us to correct your information or ask about a saved request.

Saved request, proposal and payment links may give access to your project information. Keep those links private and share them only with people you intend to have access. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle information.

3. Text messages from Backup Power Pro

If you separately choose to receive texts, you consent to messages from Backup Power Pro about your requested quote and installation. These may include a request for setup photos, answers to your questions, proposal information, scheduling and project updates. The initial photo request may be sent automatically. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Text consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase. To request a quote without texts, email backuppowerpro.network@gmail.com.

Reply STOP to opt out of further texts. Reply HELP for text-message information. For assistance with your request or project, email us at the address above.
